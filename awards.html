Awards

DARS 2026 will present Paper Awards for outstanding papers and a Student Travel Award to support student participation.

Paper Award Nominees

The following papers have been selected as nominees for the DARS 2026 Paper Awards.

Nominees

  • MACH3: Multi-Agent Cooperative Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning via a Three-Layer Architecture Alessandro Bonetti, Xin Zhou, Yifeng Zhang, Silvia Proia, Tanishq Duhan, Tingguang Zhou, Lorenzo Sabattini and Guillaume Sartoretti
  • Heterogeneous Sensor-Based Multi-Robot Exploration for Radiation Source Localization in Unknown Environments Akinori Watanabe, Yusuke Tamura and Yasuhisa Hirata
  • Experimental Evaluation of Decentralized Hemispherical Coverage for Multi-UAV Systems Mehdi Belal, Tiziano Manoni, Dario Albani and Lorenzo Sabattini
  • Safe and Energy-Aware Decentralized PDE-Constrained Optimization-Based Control of Multi-UAVs for Persistent Wildfire Suppression Longchen Niu and Gennaro Notomista
  • Probabilistic Multi-Robot Gas Source Localization with Uncalibrated Sensors: A Distributed Estimation Approach Wanting Jin, Marc Zoel Arias Mitjà and Alcherio Martinoli

Selection process: The final Paper Award recipients will be determined by the Award Committee based on both the submitted papers and the presentations delivered in S5: Award Nominees.
